enunciado: On January 10, 2025, an employer and a worker orally agree that the worker will be employed for 11 months, starting on March 1, 2025, and ending on January 31, 2026. On February 1, 2025, before any work begins, the employer tells the worker that it will not honor the agreement. The worker sues for breach. Under the statute of frauds, is the oral agreement enforceable? Options: A. Yes, because the employment lasts less than one year B. No, because by its terms it cannot be fully performed within one year of its making C. Yes, because employment contracts are exempt from the statute of frauds D. No, because every employment contract must be in writing
---
## PROGRESSION:ADVANCES
We need to decide whether an oral employment agreement made on January 10, 2025, for 11 months of work from March 1, 2025, to January 31, 2026, can be enforced. No work has been done.

## ALTERNATIVE:REJECTED | implicita
One route would be to ask whether the worker relied on the promise, for example by turning down other jobs, so that it could be enforced on that basis; but the facts show no reliance, so the question turns on the statute of frauds itself.

## PROGRESSION:ADVANCES
The statute of frauds requires a writing for an agreement that, by its terms, cannot be fully performed within one year.

## ERROR | id=e1
The employment lasts 11 months, which is less than a year, so the one-year provision does not apply and the oral agreement is enforceable.

## VERIFICATION:REFUTED | implicita
The one-year period is counted from the day the agreement is made, January 10, 2025, so it ends on January 10, 2026, while the work would continue until January 31, 2026.

## CORRECTION:RECOVERED | implicita | id=c1
So the 11-month length does not decide the question. Performance ends more than a year after the agreement was made, the agreement falls within the one-year provision, and without a writing it cannot be enforced.

## VERIFICATION:CONFIRMED | implicita | objetivo=c1
The provision asks whether the agreement could be completed within a year of its making under its own terms. The work cannot start before March 1, 2025, and 11 months from then runs past January 10, 2026, so there is no way to finish it in time.

## PROGRESSION:REACHES_OUTPUT
The oral agreement is unenforceable because by its terms it cannot be fully performed within one year of its making, option B.

Answer: B.
