enunciado: A 1,200-kg car traveling at 20 m/s brakes uniformly and comes to rest in 5 seconds. What is the magnitude of the average braking force, in newtons?
---
## PROGRESSION:ADVANCES
Need the average braking force. Mass 1,200 kg, initial speed 20 m/s, final speed 0, stopping time 5 s.

## ALTERNATIVE:REJECTED | implicita
The work-energy route would need the stopping distance, which is not given; the force-and-acceleration route needs only the stopping time, which is.

## PROGRESSION:ADVANCES
Acceleration: a = (v - v0) / t = (0 - 20) / 5 = -4 m/s^2. Magnitude 4 m/s^2.

## PROGRESSION:ADVANCES
Force: F = m a = 1,200 x 4 = 4,800 N.

## SUPPORT
Uniform braking means the acceleration is constant, so the average force over the 5 s equals the force at every instant.

## PROGRESSION:REACHES_OUTPUT
Average braking force: 4,800 N. Answer: 4800.
