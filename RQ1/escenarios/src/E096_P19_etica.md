## PROGRESSION:ADVANCES
We are given: a 70-year-old man with mild dementia refuses recommended hip surgery after an explanation of risks and benefits; he can repeat the information, gives a reason, and repeats his choice the next day. We need the appropriate response to his refusal.

## ALTERNATIVE:REJECTED | implicita
One route would hand the decision to his family: with a dementia diagnosis, his daughter would consent for him and the surgery would go ahead. Decision-making capacity, however, is specific to each decision and is judged by his abilities at the time, not by his diagnosis, so that route does not apply.

## PROGRESSION:ADVANCES | id=cap
Capacity requires that the patient understand the relevant information, appreciate how it applies to him, reason about the options, and communicate a choice. He repeats the information in his own words, explains his fear of a long recovery away from home, and states the same choice the next day, so he has the capacity to make this decision.

## PROGRESSION:ADVANCES
A patient with capacity may refuse recommended treatment, so the surgeon should respect the refusal, document the discussion, and leave the option of surgery open if he changes his mind.

## VERIFICATION:CONFIRMED | implicita | objetivo=cap
A stable choice is the usual test that a refusal is more than a passing reaction, and he gave the same answer when asked again the next day, after time to reconsider, so the finding of capacity holds.

## PROGRESSION:REACHES_OUTPUT
Thus final answer: A.
