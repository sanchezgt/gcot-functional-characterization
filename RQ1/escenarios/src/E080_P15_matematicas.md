## PROGRESSION:ADVANCES
We want the probability that two cards drawn at random from a standard 52-card deck are both aces. A standard deck has 4 aces.

## PROGRESSION:ADVANCES
The first card is an ace with probability 4/52; with that ace gone, the second is an ace with probability 3/51.

## VERIFICATION:INCONCLUSIVE | implicita
That second factor assumes the first card is not put back. The problem does not state it; drawing two cards from a deck is usually taken to mean without replacement, but the wording itself does not settle it.

## PROGRESSION:ADVANCES
Taking the draws without replacement: 4/52 x 3/51 = 12/2652 = 1/221.

## SUPPORT
The probabilities are multiplied because both draws must succeed, and the second probability is conditional on the first card having been an ace.

## PROGRESSION:REACHES_OUTPUT
The probability that both cards are aces is 1/221.

Answer: 1/221.
