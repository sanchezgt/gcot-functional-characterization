enunciado: A checking account pays no interest, while an alternative savings account pays 4% per year compounded annually. A customer moves $2,500 from checking into the savings account and plans to wait 2 years before withdrawing. How much will be in the savings account after 2 years, in dollars?
---
## PROGRESSION:ADVANCES | distractor
Need the balance after 2 years. $2,500 moved out of checking, which pays 0%, into the alternative account at 4% compounded annually; the wait is 2 years.

## PROGRESSION:ADVANCES
Year 1: 2,500 x 1.04 = 2,600.

## PROGRESSION:ADVANCES
Year 2: 2,600 x 1.04 = 2,704.

## SUPPORT
Annual compounding: the second year's interest is earned on the balance at the end of the first year, 2,600.

## PROGRESSION:REACHES_OUTPUT | distractor
Balance after the 2-year wait: $2,704. Answer: 2704.
