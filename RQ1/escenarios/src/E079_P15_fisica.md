enunciado: A 10-kg box is pushed across a floor by a horizontal force of 50 N and moves at constant velocity. What is the magnitude of the friction force acting on the box, in newtons?
---
## PROGRESSION:ADVANCES
Need the friction force on a box pushed at constant velocity by a 50 N horizontal force.

## PROGRESSION:ADVANCES
Constant velocity: acceleration zero, so net force zero (Newton's first law), and friction must balance the 50 N push.

## VERIFICATION:INCONCLUSIVE | implicita
That balance takes the floor as level, which is not stated. On a slope, a component of gravity would also act along the motion, and the data give no angle either way.

## PROGRESSION:ADVANCES
Level floor taken: the horizontal forces are the push and friction only, so friction = 50 N, opposite to the push.

## SUPPORT
The 10-kg mass does not enter, because at constant velocity the push only has to balance friction.

## PROGRESSION:REACHES_OUTPUT
Friction: 50 N. Answer: 50.
