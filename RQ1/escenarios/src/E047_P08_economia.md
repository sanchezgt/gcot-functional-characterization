enunciado: A savings account pays a nominal interest rate of 7% per year while inflation runs at 3% per year. Using the Fisher approximation, what is the real interest rate, in percent?
---
## PROGRESSION:ADVANCES
Okay, let's see. Nominal rate 7%, inflation 3%, and I need the real rate with the Fisher approximation.

## PROGRESSION:ADVANCES
The real rate measures how much purchasing power grows when the money grows at the nominal rate while prices grow at the inflation rate.

## SUPPORT
The exact relation is (1 + nominal) = (1 + real)(1 + inflation); the Fisher approximation drops the small cross term, real x inflation.

## ERROR
So the real rate is 7 + 3 = 10%.

## CORRECTION:RECOVERED | explicita
Actually, no, I added when I should have subtracted. Inflation eats into the nominal return, so the approximation is nominal minus inflation: 7 - 3 = 4%.

## PROGRESSION:REACHES_OUTPUT
So the answer should be 4.
