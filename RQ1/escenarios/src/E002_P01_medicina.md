## PROGRESSION:ADVANCES
We are given: "A 45-year-old woman comes to the physician because of 6 months of fatigue, cold intolerance, a 5-kg weight gain, and constipation. Her pulse is 54/min. Examination shows dry skin, a diffusely enlarged, nontender thyroid gland, and delayed relaxation of the ankle reflexes. Serum TSH is 18 µU/mL and free T4 is low. Which of the following is the most likely cause of her condition? Options: A. Graves disease, B. Hashimoto thyroiditis, C. Pituitary adenoma, D. Subacute granulomatous thyroiditis."

We need to identify the cause of her thyroid disorder.

## PROGRESSION:ADVANCES
Clinical clues: fatigue, cold intolerance, weight gain, constipation, bradycardia (pulse 54), dry skin, and delayed relaxation of the reflexes. All of these are features of hypothyroidism. The thyroid is diffusely enlarged and nontender.

## PROGRESSION:ADVANCES
The labs show a high TSH with a low free T4. A high TSH together with a low T4 means the pituitary is responding normally to a thyroid that is not producing enough hormone, so this is primary hypothyroidism.

## VERIFICATION:CONFIRMED | explicita
Let me double-check that reading of the labs before going on. If the problem were in the pituitary, TSH would be low or inappropriately normal, not high. Here TSH is 18, well above normal, with a low T4. That is exactly the pattern of primary hypothyroidism, so the reading holds.

## PROGRESSION:ADVANCES
The most common cause of primary hypothyroidism with a diffuse, nontender goiter in a middle-aged woman is Hashimoto thyroiditis, an autoimmune destruction of the thyroid associated with anti-thyroid peroxidase antibodies.

## SUPPORT
Hashimoto thyroiditis is a chronic lymphocytic inflammation, which is painless, and it destroys the gland gradually, over months to years.

## PROGRESSION:REACHES_OUTPUT
So the most likely cause is Hashimoto thyroiditis, option B.

Thus final answer: B.
