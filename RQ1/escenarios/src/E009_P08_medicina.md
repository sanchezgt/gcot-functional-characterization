## PROGRESSION:ADVANCES
Need the antibiotic most likely to raise the INR in a patient on warfarin. Stable INR 2.5 for a year, then 6.8 with bleeding ten days after starting an antibiotic for a UTI.

## PROGRESSION:ADVANCES
Warfarin is metabolized mainly by CYP2C9 and is highly protein bound. Drugs that inhibit CYP2C9 or displace warfarin from albumin raise the INR.

## PROGRESSION:ADVANCES
Among the options, trimethoprim-sulfamethoxazole is the classic culprit: sulfamethoxazole inhibits CYP2C9 and displaces warfarin from plasma proteins, and trimethoprim adds to the effect.

## SUPPORT
The interaction builds up over several days of therapy, as sulfamethoxazole accumulates and warfarin clearance falls.

## ERROR
So the answer is C, trimethoprim-sulfamethoxazole.
