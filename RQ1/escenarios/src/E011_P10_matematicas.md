## PROGRESSION:ADVANCES
We are given: "The length of a rectangle is 3 cm more than twice its width. The perimeter of the rectangle is 36 cm. What is the area of the rectangle?" Let the width be w. Then the length is 2w + 3.

## VERIFICATION:CONFIRMED | explicita
Let me verify the setup before using it. "3 cm more than twice its width" means twice the width, which is 2w, plus 3, so 2w + 3. Yes, the expression is right.

## PROGRESSION:ADVANCES
The perimeter of a rectangle is 2(length + width), so 2(2w + 3 + w) = 36. That gives 2(3w + 3) = 36, so 6w + 6 = 36, so 6w = 30 and w = 5.

## SUPPORT
The perimeter formula doubles the sum of length and width because each of those sides appears twice around the rectangle.

## PROGRESSION:ADVANCES
Then the length is 2 * 5 + 3 = 13 cm.
