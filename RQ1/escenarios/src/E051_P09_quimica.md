enunciado: A sample of gas occupies 10.0 L at 300 K and 2.0 atm. The temperature is raised to 600 K while the pressure is held constant. What is the new volume, in liters?

## PROGRESSION:ADVANCES
Need the volume after heating from 300 K to 600 K at constant pressure. Initial volume 10.0 L.

## PROGRESSION:ADVANCES
At constant pressure and fixed amount of gas, volume is proportional to absolute temperature: V1 / T1 = V2 / T2 (Charles's law).

## VERIFICATION:INCONCLUSIVE | explicita
Check whether the gas can be treated as ideal, since Charles's law assumes it. The problem gives no identity for the gas; at 2.0 atm and these temperatures most gases are close to ideal, but the data do not let me confirm it. Can't settle it; proceeding on the ideal-gas assumption.

## PROGRESSION:ADVANCES
V2 = V1 x T2 / T1 = 10.0 x 600 / 300 = 20.0 L.

## SUPPORT
The proportionality holds only for absolute temperature, which is why the law is written with kelvin.

## PROGRESSION:REACHES_OUTPUT
New volume: 20.0 L. Answer: 20.
