## PROGRESSION:ADVANCES
Okay, let's see. A 2-kg ball moving at 3 m/s, and I need its kinetic energy.

## PROGRESSION:ADVANCES
Kinetic energy is one half the mass times the speed squared.

## ERROR
So KE = 1/2 x 2 x 3 = 3 J.

## CORRECTION:RECOVERED | implicita
With the speed squared, 3^2 = 9, so KE = 1/2 x 2 x 9 = 9 J.

## SUPPORT
The speed is squared because the work needed to bring the ball from rest to speed v grows with v^2.

## PROGRESSION:REACHES_OUTPUT
So the answer should be 9 J.
