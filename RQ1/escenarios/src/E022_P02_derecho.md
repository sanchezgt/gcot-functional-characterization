The buyer pays a $5,000 deposit, takes possession of the land with the seller's permission, and builds a fence around it at a cost of $4,000. The seller then refuses to convey the land and argues that the agreement is unenforceable because it was never put in writing. Under the common-law statute of frauds, which of the following is the most likely result? Options: A. The agreement is unenforceable because contracts for the sale of land must be in writing B. The agreement is enforceable under the part performance exception C. The agreement is unenforceable because the deposit was less than half the price D. The agreement is enforceable because oral contracts are always binding

## PROGRESSION:ADVANCES
We are given an oral agreement to sell land for $50,000, a $5,000 deposit, the buyer's taking possession with the seller's permission, and the buyer's building of a $4,000 fence. The seller now invokes the statute of frauds.

We need to decide whether the buyer can enforce the agreement.

## PROGRESSION:ADVANCES
The statute of frauds requires contracts for the sale of land to be evidenced by a signed writing. There is no writing here, so on its face the agreement falls within the statute and would be unenforceable.

## PROGRESSION:ADVANCES
The exception that matters is part performance. Where the buyer has taken possession, paid part of the price, and made improvements to the land in reliance on the oral agreement, courts enforce the contract despite the absence of a writing, because those acts are explainable only by the existence of the contract. Here the buyer took possession, paid $5,000, and built a fence, so the exception applies.

## VERIFICATION:CONFIRMED | implicita
Set against that requirement, each act passes: it came after the oral agreement and with the seller's permission, so the possession was not a trespass, and nobody fences land at a cost of $4,000 without expecting to own it. The acts are tied to this sale and to nothing else.

## PROGRESSION:ADVANCES
So the buyer can obtain specific performance of the oral agreement.

## SUPPORT
The rationale is evidentiary: visible acts on the land serve the purpose of the missing writing by showing that a sale was agreed.

## PROGRESSION:REACHES_OUTPUT
Thus final answer: B.
