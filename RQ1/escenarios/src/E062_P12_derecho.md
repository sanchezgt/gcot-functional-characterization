## PROGRESSION:ADVANCES | distractor
Okay, let's see. A signed agreement to sell a bicycle for $500, the seller delivered it, the buyer paid by check, and the check bounced. Does the buyer still owe the money?

## PROGRESSION:ADVANCES | distractor
When a seller takes a check for the price, the buyer's obligation to pay is suspended while the check is pending. The bank verified the signature and took the check for deposit; the debt itself is discharged only if the check is actually paid.

## PROGRESSION:ADVANCES | distractor
The check was dishonored for insufficient funds. When a check taken for an obligation is dishonored, the suspension ends, and the seller can sue either on the check or on the original contract.

## SUPPORT | distractor
The seller did not have to wait for any further step: the return of the check unpaid is itself the dishonor that revives the debt.

## PROGRESSION:REACHES_OUTPUT
So the buyer still owes the $500 under the contract. The answer should be B.
