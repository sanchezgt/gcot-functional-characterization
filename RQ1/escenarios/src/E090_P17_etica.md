enunciado: A physician realizes that she gave a hospitalized patient the wrong medication. The patient has developed only mild nausea, which is expected to resolve. Which of the following is the most appropriate action for the physician? Options: A. Disclose the error to the patient now B. Disclose the error only if the patient's harm becomes serious C. Inform the patient's family but not the patient D. Document the event in the chart without telling the patient
---
## PROGRESSION:ADVANCES
We must decide what a physician should do after giving a patient the wrong medication, with only mild nausea as a result.

## PROGRESSION:ADVANCES
Professional guidance calls for honest communication with patients about errors in their care: an explanation of what happened, an apology, a description of what is being done, and a report through the institution's safety system.

## ERROR
Since the harm is minor, disclosure is required only if the nausea gets worse, so for now the physician should simply document the event.

## CORRECTION:RECOVERED | implicita
The duty to disclose covers errors that reach the patient whatever the severity of the harm, so the physician should tell the patient now what happened and what is being done about it.

## SUPPORT
Prompt disclosure respects the patient's right to know about their own care and maintains the trust on which the relationship depends.

## PROGRESSION:REACHES_OUTPUT
The physician should disclose the error to the patient now, option A.

Answer: A.
