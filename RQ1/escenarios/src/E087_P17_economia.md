## PROGRESSION:ADVANCES
We are given: "What is the present value of $1,100 to be received one year from now if the discount rate is 10%?"

We need to bring the future amount back to today.

## PROGRESSION:ADVANCES
The present value is the amount that, invested today at 10%, would grow to $1,100 in one year.

## SUPPORT
This is the time value of money: a dollar received later is worth less than a dollar today, because today's dollar can earn interest in the meantime.

## ERROR
PV = 1,100 x 1.10 = 1,210.

## CORRECTION:RECOVERED | implicita
Discounting divides by the growth factor: PV = 1,100 / 1.10 = 1,000.

## PROGRESSION:REACHES_OUTPUT
Thus final answer: 1000.
