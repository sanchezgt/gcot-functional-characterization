## PROGRESSION:ADVANCES
Okay, let's see. A kettle rated at 1,500 W on a 230-V supply, and I need the current it draws.

## PROGRESSION:ADVANCES
Electrical power, voltage, and current are related by P = V I.

## ERROR
So I = V / P = 230 / 1,500 = 0.15 A.

## CORRECTION:RECOVERED | implicita
Solving P = V I for the current puts the power on top and the voltage underneath: I = P / V = 1,500 / 230 = 6.52 A, so about 6.5 A rather than 0.15 A.

## SUPPORT
A watt is a volt-ampere, which is why dividing a power in watts by a voltage in volts yields a current in amperes.

## PROGRESSION:REACHES_OUTPUT
So the answer should be 6.5 A.
