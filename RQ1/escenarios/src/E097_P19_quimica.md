Need the oxidation state of Mn in KMnO4.

## ALTERNATIVE:REJECTED | implicita
Assigning electrons bond by bond from a Lewis structure would give it, but with K at +1 and O at -2 the sum rule needs no structure.

## PROGRESSION:ADVANCES
Neutral compound: (+1) + x + 4(-2) = 0, so x - 7 = 0 and x = +7.

## VERIFICATION:CONFIRMED | implicita
Charges: +1 for K, +7 for Mn, and -8 for the four O atoms, a total of 0, as a neutral compound requires.

## PROGRESSION:REACHES_OUTPUT
Mn: +7. Answer: +7.
