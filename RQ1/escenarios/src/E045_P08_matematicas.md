We are given: "A recipe calls for 3/4 cup of sugar per batch of cookies. How many cups of sugar are needed for 6 batches?"

We need to multiply the amount per batch by the number of batches.

## PROGRESSION:ADVANCES
Sugar needed: 6 * 3/4 = 18/4 cups.

## PROGRESSION:ADVANCES
