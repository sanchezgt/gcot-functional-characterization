enunciado: A 45-year-old woman who is a Jehovah's Witness is scheduled for elective hip surgery. After the surgeon explains that she may need a blood transfusion and the risks of refusing one, she confirms in writing that she refuses any transfusion of donated blood, and she asks about alternative techniques that avoid it. She has full decision-making capacity. Which of the following is the most appropriate action for the surgical team? Options: A. Respect the refusal and plan blood-sparing techniques B. Obtain a court order to transfuse if needed C. Plan to transfuse if her life is at risk during surgery D. Ask her husband to consent to a transfusion
---
## PROGRESSION:ADVANCES
The patient is a competent adult who has been told the risks of refusing a transfusion. We must choose the most appropriate action for the surgical team.

## PROGRESSION:ADVANCES
The governing principle is respect for autonomy: a competent adult who has been informed may refuse any treatment, including life-saving treatment, for religious or any other reasons.

## PROGRESSION:ADVANCES | distractor
Her written refusal confirms that the decision is informed and voluntary, and she has actually asked the team about alternative techniques, such as cell salvage and drugs that reduce bleeding, that avoid donated blood.

## SUPPORT | distractor
Planning those blood-sparing techniques in advance lets the team honor her decision while keeping the surgery as safe as possible, and the anesthesiologist can double-check the plan with her before the operation.

## PROGRESSION:REACHES_OUTPUT
The most appropriate action is to respect the refusal and plan blood-sparing techniques, option A.

Answer: A.
