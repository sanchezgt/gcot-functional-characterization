enunciado: Using the Cockcroft-Gault equation, estimate the creatinine clearance of a 70-year-old man who weighs 70 kg and has a serum creatinine of 1.0 mg/dL. Give the result in mL/min, rounded to the nearest whole number.
---
## PROGRESSION:ADVANCES
Need CrCl by Cockcroft-Gault: man, 70 years, 70 kg, serum creatinine 1.0 mg/dL.

## PROGRESSION:ADVANCES
CrCl = (140 - age) x weight / (72 x serum creatinine), weight in kg; weight entered as the 70 kg given.

## VERIFICATION:INCONCLUSIVE | implicita
Actual weight suits the equation only when it is close to ideal body weight. Ideal weight needs height, which is not given, so whether 70 kg should give way to an ideal or adjusted weight cannot be settled from the data.

## PROGRESSION:ADVANCES
Actual weight used: (140 - 70) x 70 / (72 x 1.0) = 4900 / 72 = 68.1 mL/min.

## SUPPORT
No 0.85 factor: it is applied only for women.

## PROGRESSION:REACHES_OUTPUT
CrCl about 68 mL/min. Answer: 68.
