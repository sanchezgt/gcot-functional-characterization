enunciado: A rigid 5.00-L container holds 0.200 mol of nitrogen gas at 27 °C. Treating the gas as ideal and using R = 0.0821 L·atm/(mol·K), what is the pressure inside the container, in atm?
---
## PROGRESSION:ADVANCES
Need the pressure of 0.200 mol N2 in 5.00 L at 27 °C. R = 0.0821 L·atm/(mol·K).

## PROGRESSION:ADVANCES
Temperature in kelvin: 27 + 273 = 300 K.

## SUPPORT
The ideal gas law takes absolute temperature: pressure is proportional to T measured from absolute zero, not from the freezing point of water.

## PROGRESSION:ADVANCES
PV = nRT, so P = nRT / V.

## PROGRESSION:ADVANCES
nRT = 0.200 x 0.0821 x 300 = 4.926 L·atm.

## PROGRESSION:ADVANCES
P = 4.926 / 5.00 = 0.985 atm.

## PROGRESSION:REACHES_OUTPUT
Pressure: about 0.985 atm. Answer: 0.985.
