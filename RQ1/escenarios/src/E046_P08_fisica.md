enunciado: A 60-watt light bulb is left on for 5 hours a day for 30 days. If electricity costs $0.20 per kilowatt-hour, how much does it cost to run the bulb for the 30 days, in dollars?
---
## PROGRESSION:ADVANCES
First, I work out how much energy the bulb uses. Its power is 60 W, which is 0.06 kW, and it runs 5 hours a day for 30 days, so 150 hours in total.

## PROGRESSION:ADVANCES
Energy is power times time: 0.06 kW x 150 h = 9 kWh.

## SUPPORT
I convert watts to kilowatts first because the price is quoted per kilowatt-hour, so the energy must be in kilowatt-hours for the units to match.

## PROGRESSION:ADVANCES
Then the cost is the energy times the price per kilowatt-hour.

## ERROR
Cost: 9 kWh x $0.20 per kWh = $18.00.

## CORRECTION:RECOVERED | explicita
Actually, I slipped a decimal place there: 9 times 0.20 is 1.80, not 18. The cost is $1.80.

## PROGRESSION:REACHES_OUTPUT
Running the bulb for the 30 days costs $1.80.

Final answer: 1.80.
