## PROGRESSION:ADVANCES
First, I recall that the number of moles is the mass divided by the molar mass, so I need the molar mass of water.

## SUPPORT
The molar mass of a compound is the sum of the atomic masses of all the atoms in its formula, each counted as many times as it appears.

## ERROR
Water is H2O, so its molar mass is 1 + 16 = 17 g/mol, and 36 g is 36 / 17 = 2.12 mol.

## CORRECTION:RECOVERED | implicita
Water has two hydrogen atoms, so its molar mass is 2 x 1 + 16 = 18 g/mol, and 36 g is 36 / 18 = 2 mol.

## PROGRESSION:ADVANCES
Each mole contains about 6.02 x 10^23 molecules, so this sample holds about 1.2 x 10^24 water molecules.

## PROGRESSION:REACHES_OUTPUT
There are 2 moles of water in 36 g.

Final answer: 2.
