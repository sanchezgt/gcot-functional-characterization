enunciado: In an insulated container, 2.0 kg of water at 80 °C is mixed with 3.0 kg of water at 20 °C. Ignoring any heat exchanged with the container and the surroundings, what is the final temperature of the mixture, in °C?
---
## PROGRESSION:ADVANCES
First, I note what is given: 2.0 kg of hot water at 80 °C and 3.0 kg of cold water at 20 °C are mixed in an insulated container, with no heat going to the container or the surroundings. I need the temperature at which the mixture settles.

## ALTERNATIVE:REJECTED | explicita
Alternatively, I could simply take the average of the two temperatures, (80 + 20) / 2 = 50 °C. But that average treats the two portions as if they had equal masses, and here there is more cold water than hot water, so the cold portion pulls the result further toward 20 °C. So I set the plain average aside.

## PROGRESSION:ADVANCES
I balance the heat instead. The heat given up by the hot water equals the heat absorbed by the cold water: 2.0 x c x (80 - T) = 3.0 x c x (T - 20), where c is the specific heat of water and T is the final temperature.

## SUPPORT
The heat balance holds because the container is insulated, so all the energy leaving the hot water must go into the cold water, and since both portions are water, the specific heat is the same on both sides.

## PROGRESSION:ADVANCES
The c cancels, leaving 160 - 2T = 3T - 60, so 5T = 220 and T = 44 °C.

## PROGRESSION:REACHES_OUTPUT
The mixture settles at 44 °C.

Final answer: 44.
