## PROGRESSION:ADVANCES
We are given: "In 2024 a country's nominal GDP is $22,000 billion and its real GDP measured in 2020 prices is $20,000 billion. By what percentage has the overall price level risen between 2020 and 2024, as measured by the GDP deflator?"

We need the GDP deflator for 2024 and then the price increase it implies relative to the base year 2020.

## PROGRESSION:ADVANCES
The GDP deflator is nominal GDP divided by real GDP, times 100.

## VERIFICATION:CONFIRMED | explicita
Let me verify the orientation of that ratio before computing. Nominal GDP values current output at current prices and real GDP values it at base-year prices, so nominal over real isolates the change in prices. In the base year the two coincide and the deflator is 100. Yes, nominal over real is correct.

## PROGRESSION:ADVANCES
Deflator for 2024: 22,000 / 20,000 x 100 = 1.10 x 100 = 110.

## ERROR
A deflator of 110 means prices have risen by 110 percent since 2020.

## VERIFICATION:REFUTED | explicita
Wait, let me check that interpretation. The deflator was 100 in the base year and is 110 now, so prices are 110 percent of their base level, which is an increase of 10 percentage points over 100, not an increase of 110 percent. The statement above confuses the level with the change.

## CORRECTION:RECOVERED | explicita
Correcting it: the price level has risen by (110 - 100) / 100 = 10 percent.

## PROGRESSION:REACHES_OUTPUT
Thus final answer: 10.
