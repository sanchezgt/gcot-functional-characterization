enunciado: On June 1 a seller mails a buyer an offer to sell a tractor. On June 2 the seller mails a letter revoking the offer. On June 3 the buyer receives the offer and mails a letter of acceptance the same day. On June 4 the buyer receives the revocation, and on June 5 the seller receives the acceptance. Under the common law, was a contract formed? Options: A. No, because the seller mailed the revocation before the buyer accepted B. Yes, a contract was formed on June 3, when the acceptance was mailed C. Yes, a contract was formed on June 5, when the acceptance arrived D. No, because the revocation arrived before the acceptance did
---
## PROGRESSION:ADVANCES
First, I put the dates in order: offer mailed June 1; revocation mailed June 2; offer received and acceptance mailed June 3; revocation received by the buyer June 4; acceptance received by the seller June 5. I need to decide whether, and when, a contract was formed.

## ALTERNATIVE:REJECTED | implicita
Dating the contract from June 5, when the acceptance arrived, would place formation after the revocation reached the buyer on June 4, and there would be no contract. For acceptances sent by mail, however, the common law looks to the moment of dispatch, so that dating does not apply.

## PROGRESSION:ADVANCES
Under that rule, the mailbox rule, an acceptance sent by a reasonable means takes effect when it is mailed, so the buyer's acceptance took effect on June 3, provided the offer was still open then.

## ERROR | id=e1
A revocation also takes effect when it is mailed, so the seller's revocation took effect on June 2, and the offer was already gone when the buyer accepted on June 3.

## VERIFICATION:REFUTED | implicita
The mailbox rule is limited to acceptances; a revocation takes effect only when the offeree receives it, and this one reached the buyer on June 4.

## CORRECTION:RECOVERED | implicita | id=c1
So the revocation did not take effect on June 2: the offer was still open on June 3, and the acceptance mailed that day formed the contract.

## VERIFICATION:CONFIRMED | implicita | objetivo=c1
The acceptance traveled by mail, the medium the seller used for the offer, and the buyer sent it only after the offer had reached him, so the dispatch rule governs it and the contract dates from June 3.

## PROGRESSION:REACHES_OUTPUT
A contract was formed on June 3, when the acceptance was mailed, option B.

Final answer: B.
