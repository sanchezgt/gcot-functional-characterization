enunciado: A store prices a jacket at 40% above its cost and later offers it at a 25% discount from that price. If the jacket cost the store $60, what is the final sale price, in dollars?
---
## PROGRESSION:ADVANCES
Okay, let's see. Cost is $60. The store marks it up 40%, then discounts that price by 25%. I need the final price.

## PROGRESSION:ADVANCES
Marked-up price: 60 plus 40% of 60. 40% of 60 is 24, so the marked-up price is 84 dollars.

## VERIFICATION:CONFIRMED | explicita
Let me double-check that markup step, since everything else depends on it. 10% of 60 is 6, so 40% is 4 times 6, which is 24, and 60 + 24 = 84. Yes, that's right.

## PROGRESSION:ADVANCES
Now the discount: 25% off 84. A quarter of 84 is 21, so the sale price is 84 - 21 = 63 dollars.

## SUPPORT
Successive percentage changes are applied one after the other, each to the result of the previous one, so the discount is taken from the marked-up price.

## PROGRESSION:REACHES_OUTPUT
So the final sale price is $63.

The answer should be 63.
