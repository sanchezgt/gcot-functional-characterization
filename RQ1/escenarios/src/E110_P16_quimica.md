enunciado: Iron reacts with oxygen to form iron(III) oxide according to the unbalanced equation Fe + O2 -> Fe2O3. When the equation is balanced with the smallest whole-number coefficients, what is the coefficient of O2?
---
## PROGRESSION:ADVANCES
Okay, let's see. I need to balance Fe + O2 -> Fe2O3 with the smallest whole numbers and give the coefficient of O2.

## PROGRESSION:ABANDONED
Write it with unknowns: a Fe + b O2 -> c Fe2O3. Iron gives a = 2c, oxygen gives 2b = 3c, and then I would pick a value for c and solve the system for a and b...

## ALTERNATIVE:ADOPTED | implicita
Oxygen is the only element whose atoms come in different bundles, 2 per O2 on the left and 3 per Fe2O3 on the right, so the smallest oxygen count both sides can hold is the least common multiple of 2 and 3, which is 6. That means 3 O2 on the left and 2 Fe2O3 on the right, and iron is then fixed by those.

## PROGRESSION:ADVANCES
2 Fe2O3 contain 4 iron atoms, so the coefficient of Fe is 4: 4 Fe + 3 O2 -> 2 Fe2O3.

## SUPPORT
Balanced equations are written with whole-number coefficients because atoms and formula units come only in whole numbers.

## PROGRESSION:REACHES_OUTPUT
So the answer should be 3: the coefficient of O2 is 3.
