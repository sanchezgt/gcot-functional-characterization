enunciado: How many grams of sodium chloride (molar mass 58.5 g/mol) are needed to prepare 250 mL of a 0.400 M aqueous solution?
---
## PROGRESSION:ADVANCES
Need grams of NaCl for 250 mL of 0.400 M. Molar mass 58.5 g/mol.

## PROGRESSION:ADVANCES
Volume in liters: 250 mL = 0.250 L.

## PROGRESSION:ADVANCES
Moles = molarity * volume = 0.400 mol/L * 0.250 L = 0.100 mol.

## PROGRESSION:ADVANCES
Mass = moles * molar mass = 0.100 mol * 58.5 g/mol = 5.85 g.

## VERIFICATION:CONFIRMED | implicita
One liter of this solution contains 0.400 mol, or 23.4 g, and a quarter liter contains a quarter of that, 5.85 g.

## PROGRESSION:REACHES_OUTPUT
Answer: 5.85 g.
