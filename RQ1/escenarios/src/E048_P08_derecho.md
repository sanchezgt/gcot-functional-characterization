enunciado: A plaintiff was injured in a car accident on March 3, 2021, and knew of the injury that same day. The applicable statute of limitations for personal injury is two years from the date the cause of action accrues. The plaintiff filed suit on March 10, 2023. Which of the following best describes the filing? Options: A. Timely, because it was filed within the limitations period B. Untimely, because the limitations period expired before the filing C. Timely, because the discovery rule extended the period D. Untimely, because the limitations period for personal injury is one year
---
## PROGRESSION:ADVANCES
Need to decide whether the March 10, 2023 filing is within a two-year limitations period that runs from accrual.

## PROGRESSION:ADVANCES
Accrual: the cause of action accrued on March 3, 2021, the day the plaintiff was injured and knew it.

## PROGRESSION:ADVANCES
Period: two years from March 3, 2021.

## ERROR
Deadline: March 3, 2024. The suit was filed March 10, 2023, well inside the period, so the filing is timely.

## CORRECTION:RECOVERED | explicita
Actually, I added three years instead of two. Two years from March 3, 2021 is March 3, 2023, not 2024. The filing on March 10, 2023 came one week after the deadline, so it is untimely.

## PROGRESSION:REACHES_OUTPUT
Untimely: the period expired on March 3, 2023, before the filing. Answer: B.
