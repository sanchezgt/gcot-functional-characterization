## PROGRESSION:ADVANCES
We are given: "A bakery sells muffins in boxes of 6. On Monday it sold 14 boxes, and on Tuesday it sold twice as many boxes as on Monday. Each muffin costs $1.50. How much money did the bakery make from muffins over the two days?"

We need the total revenue from muffins on Monday and Tuesday. The price is per muffin, so we need the number of muffins, not the number of boxes.

## PROGRESSION:ADVANCES
Monday: 14 boxes. Each box holds 6 muffins, so 14 * 6 = 84 muffins.

## PROGRESSION:ADVANCES
Tuesday: twice as many boxes as Monday, so 2 * 14 = 28 boxes. That is 28 * 6 = 168 muffins.

## PROGRESSION:ADVANCES
Total muffins over the two days: 84 + 168 = 252 muffins.

## SUPPORT
Each muffin sells for $1.50, so the revenue is the number of muffins times 1.50.

## PROGRESSION:ADVANCES
Revenue: 252 * 1.50 = 252 + 126 = 378.

## PROGRESSION:REACHES_OUTPUT
So the bakery made $378 from muffins over the two days.

Thus final answer: 378.
