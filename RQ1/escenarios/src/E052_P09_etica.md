The physician advises him to stop driving until his seizures are controlled. The patient refuses, saying he cannot afford to lose his job, and asks the physician to keep his condition confidential. Notify the driver licensing authority in accordance with the applicable reporting rules C. Inform the patient's employer directly D. Terminate the physician-patient relationship

## PROGRESSION:ADVANCES
The case: a bus driver with uncontrolled seizures refuses to stop driving and asks for confidentiality. We must choose the physician's most appropriate action.

## PROGRESSION:ADVANCES
Two duties conflict here: the duty of confidentiality to the patient and the duty to protect third parties from a serious and foreseeable danger. A bus driver who may lose consciousness at the wheel endangers many people.

## PROGRESSION:ADVANCES
Confidentiality is not absolute. When a patient poses a serious risk to others and refuses to reduce it, disclosure limited to what is necessary to avert the harm is ethically justified, and the proper recipient is the authority that controls driving privileges.

## PROGRESSION:REACHES_OUTPUT
The most appropriate action is to notify the driver licensing authority under the applicable rules, option B.

Answer: B.
