enunciado: A car starts from rest and accelerates uniformly, reaching a speed of 24 m/s after 8 seconds. How far does the car travel during these 8 seconds, in meters?
---
## PROGRESSION:ADVANCES
The car starts from rest, so its initial velocity is 0 m/s. After 8 s its velocity is 24 m/s, and the acceleration is uniform. We want the distance covered in those 8 s.

## PROGRESSION:ADVANCES
Step 1: find the acceleration. With uniform acceleration, a = (v - v0) / t = (24 - 0) / 8 = 3 m/s^2.

## PROGRESSION:ADVANCES
Step 2: find the distance. For motion from rest with uniform acceleration, d = (1/2) a t^2 = (1/2)(3)(8^2) = (1/2)(3)(64) = 96 m.

## SUPPORT
The formula d = (1/2) a t^2 applies because the initial velocity is zero; otherwise a v0 t term would be added.

## PROGRESSION:REACHES_OUTPUT
The car travels 96 meters.

Answer: 96.
