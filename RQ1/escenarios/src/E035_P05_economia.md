enunciado: A small workshop has fixed costs of $2,000 per month. Each unit it produces costs $15 in variable costs and sells for $25. How many units must it sell per month to break even?
---
## PROGRESSION:ADVANCES
Okay, let's see. Fixed costs $2,000 a month, variable cost $15 per unit, price $25 per unit. Break-even is where total revenue equals total cost.

## ALTERNATIVE:REJECTED | explicita
Alternatively, I could build a table of revenue and cost at 50 units, 100 units, 150 units, and so on, until profit crosses zero. But the break-even formula gives the exact number in one step, so I'll skip the table.

## PROGRESSION:ADVANCES
Each unit sold contributes price minus variable cost toward the fixed costs: 25 - 15 = 10 dollars per unit.

## PROGRESSION:ADVANCES
To cover $2,000 of fixed costs at $10 per unit, the workshop needs 2,000 / 10 = 200 units.

## SUPPORT
The $10 is the contribution margin: the part of each sale left over, after paying for that unit's own costs, to cover the fixed costs.

## PROGRESSION:REACHES_OUTPUT
So the answer should be 200 units.
