enunciado: When the price of a good rises from $10 to $12, the quantity demanded falls from 200 units to 160 units. Using the midpoint (arc) method, what is the price elasticity of demand? Round to two decimal places.
---
## PROGRESSION:ADVANCES
Need the price elasticity of demand by the midpoint method. Price 10 to 12, quantity 200 to 160.

## ERROR
Percent change in quantity: -40 / 200 = -20%. Percent change in price: 2 / 10 = 20%. Elasticity = -20% / 20% = -1.00.

## VERIFICATION:REFUTED | explicita
Check against the method asked for: the midpoint method divides each change by the average of the two values, not by the initial value, so the percentages above use the wrong bases.

## CORRECTION:RECOVERED | explicita
Correcting it with midpoints: average quantity (200 + 160) / 2 = 180, average price (10 + 12) / 2 = 11; quantity change -40 / 180 = -0.2222; price change 2 / 11 = 0.1818. Elasticity = -0.2222 / 0.1818 = -1.22.

## SUPPORT
The midpoint method is used so that the elasticity is the same whether the price moves from 10 to 12 or from 12 to 10.

## PROGRESSION:REACHES_OUTPUT
Elasticity = -1.22. Answer: -1.22.
