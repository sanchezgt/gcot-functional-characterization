## PROGRESSION:ADVANCES
Okay, let's see. A train covers 180 km in 2 hours, then goes at 60 km/h for 90 minutes, and we want the average speed for the whole trip. Average speed is total distance divided by total time, so I need both totals.

## PROGRESSION:ADVANCES
First leg: 180 km in 2 hours. That part is given directly.

## PROGRESSION:ADVANCES
Second leg: 90 minutes is 1.5 hours. At 60 km/h, the distance is 60 * 1.5 = 90 km.

## VERIFICATION:CONFIRMED | implicita
Taking the second leg as 60 km in the first hour plus 30 km in the remaining half hour also gives 90 km.

## PROGRESSION:ADVANCES
Total distance: 180 + 90 = 270 km. Total time: 2 + 1.5 = 3.5 hours.

## PROGRESSION:ADVANCES
Average speed: 270 / 3.5. 3.5 goes into 270 about 77 times, since 3.5 * 77 = 269.5. The remainder is 0.5, and 0.5 / 3.5 is about 0.14. So the average speed is about 77.14 km/h.

## PROGRESSION:REACHES_OUTPUT
Rounded to one decimal place, that's 77.1 km/h.

So the answer should be 77.1.
