enunciado: A country has 250 million people of working age. Of these, 190 million are employed, 10 million are without a job and actively looking for one, and the remaining 50 million are neither working nor looking for work. What is the unemployment rate, in percent?
---
## PROGRESSION:ADVANCES
Okay, let's see. 250 million people of working age: 190 million employed, 10 million jobless and looking, 50 million neither working nor looking. I need the unemployment rate.

## PROGRESSION:ADVANCES
The unemployment rate is the number of unemployed divided by the labor force, and the labor force is the employed plus the unemployed: 190 + 10 = 200 million. The 50 million who aren't looking are outside it.

## PROGRESSION:ADVANCES
Unemployment rate: 10 / 200 = 0.05, so 5%.

## VERIFICATION:CONFIRMED | implicita
From the employed side: 190 of the 200 million in the labor force have jobs, which is 95%, and the rest of the labor force, 100% - 95%, is the 5% without one. Same figure.

## SUPPORT
People who aren't looking for work are left out of the labor force because they aren't offering their labor, so counting them would understate how hard it is to find a job.

## PROGRESSION:REACHES_OUTPUT
So the unemployment rate should be 5%.
