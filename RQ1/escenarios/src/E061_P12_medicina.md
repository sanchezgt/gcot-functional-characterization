## PROGRESSION:ADVANCES
The question: a 27-year-old woman at 10 weeks' gestation, never vaccinated against measles, mumps, and rubella, asks which vaccines she can receive and when she can have the MMR vaccine.

We need to decide when she should receive the MMR vaccine.

## PROGRESSION:ADVANCES
MMR is a live attenuated vaccine. Live vaccines are contraindicated during pregnancy because of the theoretical risk that the attenuated virus infects the fetus.

## PROGRESSION:ADVANCES | distractor
Her pregnancy was confirmed by ultrasound at the check-up, so she is pregnant now, at 10 weeks, and the contraindication applies for the rest of the pregnancy. The vaccine has to wait until after delivery.

## SUPPORT | distractor
The postpartum visit is the usual checkpoint for catching up on live vaccines, and MMR can be given right after delivery, even while she is breastfeeding.

## PROGRESSION:REACHES_OUTPUT
So the recommendation is to give the MMR vaccine after delivery, option B.

Thus final answer: B.
