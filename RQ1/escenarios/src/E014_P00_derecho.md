enunciado: A painter offers in writing to paint a homeowner's house for $5,000. The homeowner replies in writing, "I accept, provided you also paint the fence for the same price." The painter does not respond and never begins work. Under the common law of contracts, which of the following best describes the legal situation? Options: A. A contract was formed when the homeowner accepted B. No contract was formed because the homeowner's reply was a counteroffer that the painter never accepted C. A contract was formed because the painter's silence constituted acceptance D. No contract was formed because the price was never agreed upon
---
## PROGRESSION:ADVANCES
Need to decide whether a contract was formed. Offer: paint the house for $5,000. Reply: "I accept, provided you also paint the fence for the same price." Painter silent, no work.

## PROGRESSION:ADVANCES
Under the common-law mirror-image rule, an acceptance must match the terms of the offer. A reply that adds a condition is not an acceptance; it is a counteroffer, and a counteroffer rejects the original offer.

## PROGRESSION:ADVANCES
The homeowner's reply adds a new term, painting the fence at no extra charge. So it is a counteroffer, and the painter's $5,000 offer is terminated by it.

## PROGRESSION:ADVANCES
A counteroffer needs its own acceptance. The painter said nothing and did nothing. Silence is not acceptance absent a prior course of dealing or a duty to speak, and nothing in the facts creates one.

## SUPPORT
The rule exists so that each party is bound only to terms it has assented to; an added term changes the bargain the offeror proposed.

## PROGRESSION:REACHES_OUTPUT
No contract: the reply was a counteroffer and it was never accepted. Answer: B.
