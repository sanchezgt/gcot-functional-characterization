## PROGRESSION:ADVANCES
Need the change from $20 after buying 3 notebooks at $4 and 2 pens at $1.50.

## PROGRESSION:ADVANCES
Notebooks: 3 * 4 = 12 dollars.

## ERROR
Pens: 2 * 1.50 = 2.50 dollars.

## VERIFICATION:REFUTED | implicita
Two pens at one dollar fifty each come to one dollar fifty plus one dollar fifty, which is three dollars, not two fifty.

## CORRECTION:RECOVERED | implicita
Pens: 3.00 dollars.

## PROGRESSION:ADVANCES
Total spent: 12 + 3 = 15 dollars.

## PROGRESSION:ADVANCES
Change: 20 - 15 = 5 dollars.

## PROGRESSION:REACHES_OUTPUT
Answer: 5.
